# D09: Series Families — Data Provenance Index

## What this covers
The per-series DPRs and EPRs for all 118 series live in `docs/series/`
(one `{SID}_DPR.md` and one `{SID}_EPR.md` per series). This document
indexes them by book chapter so a reader can find a family fast.

Every per-series DPR documents: source, construction steps,
transformations, known issues and validation. The Anu registry
(`anu/series_registry.json`) carries the machine-readable twin of
this map (`documentation.dpr` per series).
