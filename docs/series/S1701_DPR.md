## 1. Definition

US and UK wholesale price indexes (WPI) expressed in ounces of gold,
smoothed with a Hodrick-Prescott filter (smoothing parameter 100), plus the
average-of-past-two-waves overlay used to project the 2008-2018 crisis.
Appears in Shaikh (2016) as **Figure 17.1** (p. 749); reuses the data already
plotted as Fig 16.1.

Three component subseries (labelled -A, -B, -C):
- **S1701-A** US PPI in gold (HP-100) — `USPPIGOLDHP100`, 1800-2007
- **S1701-B** UK PPI in gold (HP-100) — `UKPPIGOLDHP100`, 1801-2007
- **S1701-C** USUKAVGWAVE overlay — 1983-2025 (post-2011 portion = forecast)

## 2. Why it matters in Chapter 17

Fig 17.1 introduces Shaikh's long-wave thesis: 1897-1939 and 1939-1983 are
two completed long waves, each with general crises 8-9 years after the peak.
The dashed average-wave overlay projected the 2008-2018 crisis interval —
a prediction Shaikh first made in 2003.

## 7. Caveats

1. **Series stub-vs-figure rename.** An earlier replication assigned the
   identifier S102 to the IRS table; Fig 17.1 is the long-wave chart. S1701
   was renamed to match the figure; IRS lineage captured in S1702/S1703.
2. **HP-filter padding undocumented in Shaikh.** Boundary-year smoothing
   choices depend on padding method; we use the workbook's stored values
   verbatim and do not re-smooth.
3. **Forecast layer.** Post-2011 USUKAVGWAVE values are Shaikh's projection,
   not data; preserved with `is_forecast` flag.
4. **MeasuringWorth WPI extension URL is dead** (returned 404). Substitute
   National Bureau of Economic Research (NBER) m04051a + BLS WPU00000000
   documented but not implemented in v1.
