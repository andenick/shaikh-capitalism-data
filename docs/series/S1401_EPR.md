# S1401 — Extension Provenance Record

**Series**: S1401 (Nominal GDP Growth and Level of Wage Share)
**Chapter**: 14
**Authored**: 2026-05-18

## Classification
- `content_type: time_series`
- `construction: composite`
- Extension applies (both subseries are composites of live U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis National Income and Product Accounts (NIPA) inputs available via FRED).

## Method
**Re-derivation, not splice.** For 2012+ the loader fetches FRED `GDP` and `W209RC1`
annually and applies Shaikh's exact formulas:

```
wagesh_t = W209RC1_t / GDP_t
ggdp_t   = GDP_t / GDP_{t-1} - 1
```

`W209RC1` is **total** Compensation of Employees (NIPA Table 1.10, line 2 — wages,
salaries AND employer supplements), the SAME series S1403 uses at quarterly
frequency. A concept guard in the loader now
rejects any wage-and-salary-only substitute at load time.

**Prior-defect note (T1.1 remediation, 2026-07-01):** the loader previously fetched
`A576RC1` (Compensation of Employees, Received: Wage & Salary Disbursements — NIPA
T2.1, ~20% low because it omits employer supplements). That understated the extension
wage share (2012 ≈ 0.426 instead of ≈ 0.527) and produced a spurious -22.2% downward
break at the 2011->2012 splice. Corrected to `W209RC1`.

Re-derivation (not growth-rate splice) is mandatory here because wage share is a
ratio of two non-stationary aggregates; a lazy splice on the ratio would freeze a
2011 numerator/denominator coupling that drifts in reality.

## Worked example
2024 (current FRED vintage): FRED GDP = 29,298.0 (billions USD seasonally adjusted annual rate (SAAR), annual avg);
FRED W209RC1 = 15,027.1 (annual). Therefore wage share 2024 = 15,027.1 / 29,298.0 ≈
0.5129; ggdp 2024 = (29,298.0 / 27,811.5) - 1 ≈ 0.0534. (Numbers drift ~1% with each
BEA NIPA vintage; freeze an ALFRED snapshot for exact reproduction.)

## No-Proxy disclosure
**No proxies used.** Both extension subseries are the EXACT BEA NIPA Table 1.10
series Shaikh used (total compensation of employees = line 2, W209RC1; gross
domestic product = line 1, GDP), delivered through FRED's BEA mirror. No
PPI-for-CPI, no earnings-for-compensation, no wage-and-salary-for-total-compensation
type substitutions (the last was exactly the A576RC1 defect the T1.1 remediation removed).

## No-Synthetic disclosure
**No synthetic, estimated, placeholder, frozen, or interpolated values.** Every
year 1948-2025 traces to a primary BEA observation (book period via
Appendix 14.3, extension via FRED API).
