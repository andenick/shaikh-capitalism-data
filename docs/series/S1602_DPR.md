## 1. Definition

**S1602** is the annual co-evolution of hourly **productivity** and hourly **real compensation** in the US Nonfarm Business Sector, 1947-2024, **indexed to 1982 = 100**, plus a Shaikh-original **counterfactual real-compensation path (`ec_c`)** estimated from OLS regression of `ln(ec) ~ ln(y) + time` over the "golden age" 1960-1981 and projected forward. The chart appears as **Figure 16.3** ("Hourly Real Wages and Productivity, US Business Sector, 1947–2012 (1982 = 100)"; the in-text description at p.730 phrases it as the relation between hourly productivity and hourly real compensation "from 1947 to 2008") and shows the post-Reagan-era divergence between productivity growth and real-wage growth.

## 2. Why it matters in Chapter 16

Chapter 16's narrative pivot is the "great divorce" of real wages from productivity in the early 1980s — a structural shift Shaikh attributes to the neoliberal policy turn. The counterfactual `ec_c` quantifies the wage path that would have prevailed under the 1960-1981 wage-productivity trend, and the gap between actual `ec` and counterfactual `ec_c` is the empirical anchor for Fig 16.4 (counterfactual profit-rate path used by S1604).

## 4. Construction

S1602 is **composite**. The shipped chopped uses Shaikh's distributed Appendix 16.2 values directly (no re-derivation), emitting the 1982=100 book series (A/B/C) plus the 1958=100 cross-chapter-cadence rebases (G/H). The Phase-6 extension procedure — re-fetch BLS via FRED at 2017=100 base, rebase to 1982=100 using overlap arithmetic at the splice year 2012, and re-run the counterfactual regression forecast — is **deferred** and not present in the shipped chopped (see §3).

**Counterfactual regression** (replicated explicitly per Phase 4 Q4):
```
window:           1960-1981 (Shaikh's "golden age")
specification:    ln(ec_t) = alpha + beta * ln(y_t) + gamma * t
fit:              OLS; gamma typically not statistically significant
forecast:         ec_c_t = exp( alpha_hat + beta_hat * ln(y_t) + gamma_hat * t )
                  for t = 1982, 1983, ..., 2024
pre-1982:         ec_c_t = ec_t (identity); counterfactual binds at 1982 by construction
```

The regression coefficients are emitted as a sidecar JSON for replication audit.

## 7. Caveats

1. **bls.org typo in book footnote**. The Appendix 16.1 footnote on p. 898 says `http://www.bls.org` — a typo for `http://www.bls.gov`. RSCD canonicalises bls.gov throughout.
2. **BLS rebase 2012 -> 2017**. BLS rebased PRS84006xxx indices to 2017=100 in 2022. The extension rebases back to 1982=100 via overlap at 2012 (last full Appendix 16.2 year). Pre/post-2012 splice continuity is validated.
3. **Productivity revisions**. BLS revises productivity quarterly; minor level shifts at the splice are expected and absorbed by the overlap-anchor reindex.
4. **Counterfactual specification choice**. The regression `ln(ec) ~ ln(y) + time` is Shaikh's published spec. The time coefficient is rarely significant, but is retained to match his Stata code; documented as a methodological choice, not a proxy.
5. **No proxies**. PRS84006093 / PRS84006153 are reachable via FRED COMPRNFB / OPHNFB and via direct data.bls.gov endpoints. The Phase 4 URL substitutions are routing changes, not concept changes.
