## Method
This build replicates the published constrained b=1 fits; a later enrichment pass
could re-fit on 1949-1982 and (extended) 1994-2024, plus a separate 1994-2007 era-2 fit for
regime-stability assessment per the data-adequacy review.

## Dual Phillips fit
BOTH variants are emitted to processed data file:
- `constrained_b1`: replicates Shaikh's published form `y = a + x^c`
- `unconstrained`: free three-parameter `y = a + b * x^c`
Comparison documented in the validator report.
