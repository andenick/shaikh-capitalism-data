## 1. Definition

**S1006** is the classic panel of annual nominal **total returns on US financial assets** (large-company
stocks, long-term corporate bonds, long-term government bonds, plus Treasury bills and inflation), 1926 onward:

- Large Company Stocks (S&P 500 since 1957; S&P 90 1926–1956) — `rslarge`
- Long-Term Corporate Bonds (~20yr AAA/AA index) — `rbcorplt`
- Long-Term Government Bonds (~20yr UST) — `rbgovlt`
- 3-month Treasury Bills — `rbtbills` (also captured)
- Inflation — `inflrate` (captured)

**Public data surface (Decision 0010).** The 1926–2010 historical reference is the Ibbotson SBBI 2004
Yearbook (Table 2-2), a **commercially licensed** dataset (now a Morningstar product). Per Decision 0010 the
SBBI columns are marked `publish:false` and are **retained internally under license, not redistributed**. The
public data surface instead uses the **open Aswath Damodaran (NYU Stern) alternates**, which are concept-matched
and openly redistributable. The book values reported in Shaikh's Table 10.1 (see §8) are a genuine published
book table and are reproduced exactly.

## 2. Why it matters

Shaikh's Figure 10.10 sets up the cross-asset return comparison that motivates the corporate incremental-rate-of-profit (IROP) vs. equity-return analysis in S1007.

## 7. Caveats

1. **Ibbotson licensing (Decisions 0005 and 0010).** SBBI is a commercially licensed dataset (now a Morningstar product), so its values cannot be redistributed in an open replication. Per Decision 0010 the SBBI columns are marked `publish:false` and the public data surface uses the open Damodaran-NYU alternates. The local `Appendix10_Ibbotson.xlsx` workbook is retained **internally under license** as the historical reference for the 1926–2010 reproduction; it is not part of the public bundle.
2. **LT Corporate Bond reconstruction is a PROXY.** Damodaran does not publish a long-corporate total-return series. The extension reconstructs an approximate total return from FRED `AAA` yield (treating the yield as a rough proxy for the running yield component of a duration-stable LT corporate index). This is flagged `proxy: true` with a Concept Match Justification in the EPR.
3. **2004–2010 segment.** The book's `Appendix10_Ibbotson.xlsx` includes Ibbotson's 2004–2010 update (attributed to David Stubbs). We use these values as-is for book replication.
