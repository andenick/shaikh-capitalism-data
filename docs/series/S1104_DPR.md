## 1. Definition

Three-line overlay chart for the United States, 1960-2009 (Figure 11.7,
p. 534):

- **S1104-A** US trade balance, net-trade ratio `(X-M)/(X+M)`.
  Phase 4 unit correction: CD2 negative sample values confirm this is the
  net-trade ratio, NOT the X/M ratio that S1101 uses.
- **S1104-B** US REER PPI-basis (same data as S1102-B; emitted here for
  self-contained chopped CSV).
- **S1104-C** US/EU12 relative real GDP, index 2002=100. Built 2026-07-02
  (FU-1) from World Bank WDI `NY.GDP.MKTP.KD` (real GDP, constant 2015 USD)
  for the US and Shaikh's FIXED pre-1995 EU12 basket; US real GDP divided by
  the summed EU12 real GDP, rebased so 2002=100. All three lines now ship.

## 2. Why it matters in Chapter 11

Figure 11.7 demonstrates that "the real exchange rate and relative GDP do
indeed pull in opposite directions" (p. 534). The deterioration of the US
trade balance is consistent with the improvement in its average competitive
level — a non-PPP fundamental driving long-run trade balances. The three
built lines corroborate this: over 1960-2009 the REER (S1104-B) falls
(≈188→73), relative GDP (S1104-C) rises (≈89→106), and the net-trade balance
(S1104-A) worsens (≈+0.09→−0.21) — REER and relative GDP move oppositely,
exactly as Shaikh states.

## 4. Construction

`composite`. Three components share an x-axis (year) but no algebraic
combination — they overlay as separate lines. S1104-C construction:
`index[y] = (US_realGDP[y] / Σ EU12_realGDP[y]) / (US_realGDP[2002] / Σ EU12_realGDP[2002]) × 100`,
all real GDP in constant 2015 USD. The EU12 basket is held FIXED (per-country
backfill, not an EU/Euro-Area aggregate).

## 5. Year coverage

## 7. Caveats

1. **Within-chapter unit inconsistency vs S1101-A.** S1101 = X/M; S1104-A
   = (X-M)/(X+M). Shaikh's authorial choice; preserved per Phase 4
   resolution Q3.
2. **S1104-C EU12 basket fixed to Shaikh's pre-1995 12 members.** Phase 4
   resolution Q5: hold basket fixed via per-country backfill, not splice
   to Euro Area. Implemented as such in L01_S1104.
3. **S1104-C source is WDI, not Shaikh's original.** Appendix 11.1.II lists
   `RelGDPR` among raw inputs but names no source; RSCD reconstructs the line
   from WDI real GDP (constant 2015 USD). It is concept-faithful to the
   figure's intent but is not a byte-for-byte reproduction of Shaikh's own
   RelGDPR series (see §9). Live-API series: WDI revisions may shift values.
