## 1. What it is (book definition + figure)

S1605 is the annual ratio of US **household debt to disposable personal income**, 1975–2011. It is **Figure
16.9** ("Household Debt-to-Income Ratio, United States, 1975-2011"). Shaikh's own narrative
(`research.book_quotes[0]`, p. 735): *"Households, whose real incomes had been squeezed by the slowdown in
real wage growth, were offered ever-cheaper debt in order to maintain growth in consumer spending. In
consequence, as shown in figure 16.9, the household debt-to-income ratio grew dramatically in the 1980s,
which added fuel to the boom."*

S1605 is the **household-side analogue** to the corporate net-profit-rate trajectory of S1604: as wage
suppression (S1602) squeezed real income, households substituted debt for income, building the leverage that
culminated in 2007 (`S1605_DPR.md` §1–2). It is paired in the book's narrative with S1606 (debt-service
ratio) on the same DPI denominator family.

(Flow of Funds TD3, line 2); HHDispPersInc = Household Disposable Personal Income (T2.1, line 27);
HHDebtIncRatio = HHDebt/HHDispPersInc."*

| Input | Source | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **HHDebt** (numerator) | Federal Reserve **Z.1 Table D.3 line 2** (household-sector total debt) → FRED **HCCSDODNS** | S1605-A |

## 3. Why these sources — Shaikh's rationale + the reference-grade concept restoration

- **Why household-only debt (Z.1 D.3 line 2), not household+nonprofit.** Shaikh's figure is a *household*
  leverage story; his Appendix names Z.1 D.3 line 2, the households-only debt line
  (`research.book_quotes[1]`). The correct FRED mirror is **HCCSDODNS** (Households; Debt Securities and
  Loans; Liability) — households only (`S1605_DPR.md` §4).
- **Why Disposable Personal Income (T2.1 line 27), not broader Personal Income.** The denominator must be the
  *after-tax* income households can actually service debt out of. Shaikh's spec is explicit: T2.1 line 27,
  **Disposable** PI. The FRED mirror is **DPI** (`research.methodology_notes[2]`; `S1605_DPR.md` §4).
- **THE REFERENCE-GRADE CONCEPT RESTORATION (review POS1).** CD2's extension used **wrong-concept proxies on
  both** numerator and denominator: **CMDEBT** (households **+ nonprofit organizations**) for the numerator
  and **PI** (broader **pre-tax** Personal Income) for the denominator (`research.methodology_notes[2]`;
  `research.open_questions[0]`; `S1605_DPR.md` §4). Both violate the anu-framework No-Proxies rule. RSCD
  **restores Shaikh's stated book spec** — `HCCSDODNS` (households-only) / `DPI` (after-tax) — with
  `proxy: false` and an explicit `proxy_justification` recording that the substitution *corrects* the CD2
  proxy rather than introducing a new one (`S1605_EPR.md` §5; `CH16_review.POS1`). This is the model
  remediation of the exact anu-framework Unit-Documentation failure mode.
- **The documented 1000× dimensional guard.** `HCCSDODNS` is published in **millions** USD; `DPI` in
  **billions** USD SAAR. The naive ratio `HCCSDODNS/DPI` is **1000× too large** — the textbook
  CMDEBT/DPI failure mode. The canonical loader carries an inline dimensional-analysis comment and computes
  `ratio = hccsdodns / (dpi * 1000.0)`, plus a **sanity assertion `0.3 ≤ ratio ≤ 1.5`** that raises
  `AssertionError` on any unit slip (`P02_S1605.py`; `S1605_DPR.md` §4 + §7 caveat 1; `S1605_EPR.md` §3;
  `CH16_review.POS1`). Households can peak near ~1.35 of DPI (2007) but never 1000×.
- **Rejected alternatives:** CMDEBT (adds nonprofits, ~6% high), PI (pre-tax, ~12–14% high), and TDSP (a
  debt-*service* ratio, a different concept — that is S1606, not S1605) (`research.extension_candidates`;
  `S1605_DPR.md` §7 caveats 3–4).

## 4. Methodological-change exposure (NIPA denominator + Fed Z.1 restructure)

S1605's denominator is a **BEA NIPA** magnitude and its numerator a **Fed Z.1** magnitude, so both
provider calendars bear on it:

- **DPI (NIPA T2.1 line 27) and the comprehensive updates.** DPI is restated at each BEA comprehensive
  update. The **2013 (14th)** update's accrual accounting for defined-benefit pensions and the personal
  income/saving revisions move DPI; **2018 (15th)** revised personal saving again; **2023 (16th)** re-based to
  reference year 2017 (`NIPA_CHANGE_TIMELINE.md` 2013/2018/2023 rows). Shaikh's ~2011 vintage predates all
  three, so a live DPI re-fetch must not be spliced across a boundary.
- **Fed Z.1 restructure (2013): Flow of Funds → Financial Accounts.** The Federal Reserve renamed the release
  and **restructured Table D.3**, so the book's "D.3 line 2" line number is no longer stable
  (`S1605_DPR.md` §7 caveat 2; `research.open_questions[1]`). RSCD sidesteps this by keying on the FRED
  series id **HCCSDODNS**, which preserves the households-only concept regardless of D.3 line renumbering.
- **Quarterly → annual.** Both components are quarterly; annualised by simple mean per Shaikh's convention
  (`S1605_DPR.md` §5).
- **No I-O touch.** Unlike S1604, S1605 does not run through the profit-rate/benchmark-I-O machinery.

## 6. Forward risk

- **Keep the sanity-band guard live at extension.** The `0.3 ≤ ratio ≤ 1.5` assertion is the trap that
  catches any future unit slip (millions vs billions); never remove it, and re-verify the `* 1000.0` factor
  and dimensional comment survive refactors (`S1605_EPR.md` §§3,9).
- **Do NOT regress to CD2's CMDEBT/PI.** The book spec is HCCSDODNS/DPI; a well-meaning "simplification" back
  to CMDEBT/PI would re-introduce the wrong-concept proxy (~7–9% level error) the framework just fixed
  (`S1605_EPR.md` §8; `research.open_questions[0]`).
- **Z.1 line renumbering + NIPA re-benchmark.** Key on FRED series ids, not Z.1 line numbers; re-derive DPI on
  one coherent NIPA vintage and verify splice continuity at 2012 within ±2% (`S1605_DPR.md` §7 caveats 2–3;
  `research.open_questions[1]`).
- **Emit the CD2 divergence diagnostic.** Report the CMDEBT/PI vs HCCSDODNS/DPI delta as informational
  evidence of the wrong-concept-proxy effect (`S1605_EPR.md` §8).
