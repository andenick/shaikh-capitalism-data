## 1. What it is (book definition + figure)

S1403 is the **quarterly HP(100) phase diagram** of Figure 14.12 (book p. 666): the U.S. wage share
(y-axis) plotted against unemployment intensity (x-axis) with time implicit, 1948–2011. Shaikh's caption
(`research.book_quotes[0]`, p. 666): *"The empirical equivalent shown in figure 14.12 is in terms of
quarterly HP(100) filtered data for the US wage share and unemployment intensity from 1948 to 2011. It is
strikingly similar to the theoretical path of the classical model…"* It is the empirical analogue of the
theoretical Goodwin-type spiral of Fig 14.9, tracing a **clockwise** loop: the wage share rises when
intensity is low and falls when intensity is high (`research.methodology_notes[2]`). Published values ship
from **Appendix 14.3**; the canonical processed parquet collapses quarterly HP100 to annual means, with a
quarterly sidecar at `Technical/data/processed/_sidecars/S1403_quarterly.parquet`
(`CH14_RESEARCH_SUMMARY.md` line 95).

## 3. Why these sources — Shaikh's rationale + rejected alternatives

- **Why total compensation over GDI-based wage-share proxies.** The extension candidate FRED
  `W270RE1Q156NBEA` was a *Shares of GDI: Compensation of employees* series — a **GDI** denominator, not
  GDP. `research.extension_candidates[0].concerns` flags this: *"Shaikh denominator is GDP, not GDI… build
  A576RC1/GDP directly."* The build correctly rejected the GDI share and reconstructed EC/GDP from the T1.10
  lines themselves (`W209RC1 + GDP`), preserving Shaikh's GDP-denominator concept.
- **Why total Compensation (T1.10 line 2), NOT wage & salary disbursements.** Same rationale as S1401 §3:
  the wage share must count *all* labor compensation (wages + supplements) to be the classical share of
  value added. S1403's extension used `W209RC1` correctly — which is precisely why it is the model the
  S1401 fix must copy.
- **Why quarterly.** The phase-portrait "zigs and zags" that make the empirical spiral resemble the
  theoretical one are only visible at quarterly frequency; footnote 10 (p. 666,
  `research.methodology_notes[1]`) notes annual HP data "also shows a clockwise spiral but… is much
  smoother [and] loses the zigs and zags."
- **Why HP(100), not HP(1600).** The chapter default is 100 for *all* frequencies (Appendix 14.2 p. 893).
  The build enforces **λ=100 even at quarterly frequency**, explicitly *not* the textbook quarterly λ=1600
  (`CH14_RESEARCH_SUMMARY.md` line 95; `HP_LAMBDA_CH14=100`).

## 4. Methodological-change exposure — dead-URL substitution + NIPA drift

- **Dead FRED URL (F10, honest and correct).** `W270RE1Q156NBEA` returned **HTTP 404** (FRED retired the
  series). The build substituted `W209RC1` + `GDP` — the *same* NIPA T1.10 lines 2 and 1 at quarterly
  frequency — documented in `series_registry.json S1403.url_substitution` with verified-at date and
  rationale ("within-FRED routing update; not a concept substitution")
  (`CH14_review.F10`; `CH14_RESEARCH_SUMMARY.md` line 116). Contrast this *correct* within-concept
  reroute with S1401's *wrong* cross-concept fetch.
- **NIPA comprehensive revisions.** Same exposure as S1401: 2013 (+$400B GDP, IPP capitalization), 2018,
  2023 all restate both EC and GDP (`NIPA_CHANGE_TIMELINE.md`). A quarterly extension must re-fetch on one
  coherent vintage; no ALFRED pin is set.
- **UEMPMEAN 2011 top-coding** propagates into the quarterly intensity x-axis exactly as in S1402.
