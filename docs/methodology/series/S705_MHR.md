## 1. What the series is
S705 is **Shaikh's own reconstruction of the US 30-industry average rate of profit**, 1987–2005 (Fig 7.15), with the aggregate ("All Private") defined by the overall profit rate of all *included* private industries. Book definition (page-cited, `S705_research.json`, p. 305): *"The first chart in figure 7.15 presents the evidence on average profit rates from 1987 to 2005 for thirty US industries. It is apparent that the previously noted patterns recur: rates of profit on total capital cluster around some central tendency, but a substantial number remain persistently above or below the average (defined by the overall profit rate of all included private industries)."* The profit-rate concept (Appendix 7.1, p. 857): ROP = **nominal net profits (gross profit − depreciation) ÷ current-cost net capital stock**, "since the US BEA now only calculates net capital stock." It is the empirical heart of the chapter's average-rate tier and the parent of the deviation panel S709 (Fig 7.16).

## 2. Source lineage — the full adjustment chain
- **Primary agency data (BEA, NAICS-native)**: BEA **GDP-by-Industry** tables (`GDPbyInd_VA_NAICS`, 1947–97 and 1998–2005) supplying Gross Value Added, Employee Compensation, **Gross Operating Surplus (GOS)**, FTPE, self-employed persons (SEP), full-time-equivalent employees (FEE), by NAICS industry (Appendix 7.1 §III.1, book p. 857). Capital side: BEA **Fixed Asset Tables 3.1ES** (current-cost net stock by industry), **3.4ES** (current-cost depreciation), **3.7ES** (historical-cost investment), **3.8ES** (chain-type quantity index).
- **The Shaikh adjustment chain** (this is *why* it is `composite`, book pp. 302–303; formula in `S705_research.json`):
  1. **WEQ (wage-equivalent of self-employed)**: PG = GOS − WEQ, where WEQ_i = w_i × SEP_i and w_i = EC_i/FEE_i. Removes the implicit wage of proprietors/partners from operating surplus. Magnitude: **Construction's measured rate falls 90.5% → 20.7%** (book p. 302).
  2. **OOH (owner-occupied housing) removal**: strips imputed homeowner-as-landlord income/stock from Real Estate — **~55.5% of imputed GOS and ~76% of imputed K** removed (book p. 302), via **NIPA Table 7.12** (imputations, lines 133–136/140) + Fixed Asset Tables 5.1/5.7.
  3. **Normal-inventory additions** to K for Manufacturing / Wholesale-Retail / Construction, via **NIPA T1BU + T2AUI** and the **Economic Census of Construction** (Table 3).
  4. **Bank/insurance reserve additions** to K for financial sectors, via **Flow of Funds** L.109 (commercial banks), L.114 (savings), L.115 (credit unions), L.116 (P&C insurance), L.117 (life). Magnitude: **Banking's measured rate falls 41.8% → 17.7%** (book p. 303).
  5. **Industry exclusion**: 31 of the 61 NAICS private industries dropped → **30 retained** (see §4), redefining the overall/aggregate rate.
- **Native units**: rate (decimal). **Aggregate-before-ratio**: yearly average ROP = Σᵢ(PGᵢ−DEPᵢ) / Σᵢ Kᵢ(−1) across the 30 retained industries — NOT an unweighted mean of industry rates (book p. 305; `S705_research.json` methodology_notes).
- **RSCD transcription vehicle**: Shaikh's online **Appendix 7.2 sheet `ropdataUSind`** = `SalvagedInputs/book_data/ShaikhChoppedTables/Appendix7_ropdataUSind.xlsx`. Crucially, **this workbook already contains the adjusted, already-reduced 30-industry panel** — the WEQ/OOH/inventory/reserve arithmetic and the exclusion are **baked in by Shaikh**, not recomputed by RSCD code (review M3, touchpoint S705). The loader `Technical/code/L01_loaders/L01_S705.py` + `_ch7_xlsx_panels.py` transcribe it byte-exact.

## 3. Why these sources (Shaikh's perspective) + rejected alternatives
Shaikh built this series himself (Shaikh 2008) because **no off-the-shelf source publishes a competitively-clean, cross-industry-comparable US profit rate**. His design choices are theory-driven:
- **BEA GDPbyIndustry (NAICS) + Fixed Assets** is the only US source giving GOS *and* industry capital stock on a consistent post-1987 basis — the minimum needed for an average rate by industry.
- **Net-stock ROP** is forced on him: BEA discontinued gross capital stock, so he defines the average rate on current-cost **net** stock (Appendix 7.1, p. 857) — and, distrusting the "debatable assumptions needed to estimate capital stocks," he pairs it with the capital-stock-free **incremental** rate (S706) as the cleaner equalization test.
- **WEQ/OOH/inventory/reserve** are non-optional *for cross-industry comparability*: without them Construction (90.5%) and Banking (41.8%) profit rates are artefacts of NIPA imputation conventions, not real competitive returns. Shaikh's whole claim — that regulating capitals equalize *real* profit rates — requires first removing these accounting distortions.
- **The 31-industry exclusion** keeps the sample to "industries dominated by profit-driven enterprises and also competitive on a world scale" (book p. 858), i.e. the domain where the theory of regulating capital actually applies.
**Rejected alternatives** (`S705_research.json` extension_candidates, Shaikh-consistent): using **raw NIPA GOS** (rejected — proprietor income + OOH + reserves distort it); **post-2013 Fixed Assets including R&D/IP** (rejected for the book vintage — changes the capital concept, §4); splicing across BEA NAICS rebasings (rejected — re-run end-to-end, never splice).

## 4. Methodological-change exposure — the prime concordance case
S705 is **the** industry-classification-intensive series of the chapter. Exposure on three axes:
- **SIC↔NAICS break**: S705 is **NAICS-native from birth** (BEA GDPbyInd_VA_NAICS, 1987 onward). It therefore *sits entirely on the NAICS side* of the 1992-SIC/1997-NAICS wall documented in `_timelines/IO_CHANGE_TIMELINE.md` — but its 30-industry sample must be tracked across **NAICS revisions** (1997→2002→2007→2012→2017→2022), each of which re-orders/re-aggregates industries. The official Census bridges for this are staged at `Technical/docs/methodology/concordances/_sources/naics/` (`1987_SIC_to_1997_NAICS`, `1997/2002/2007/2012/2017/2022` chains) per `_sources/SOURCES.md`; the project's Ch7-specific stability table is `Inputs/Capitalism Data/Technical/Divergence_Reports/ADR-005_NAICS/data/naics_concordance_master.csv` (NAICS 2002–2022 change/stability flags for exactly this 30-industry sample). Shaikh's own aggregation quirks (combine sectors 50–51 and 69–70 to match GDPbyInd boundaries) must be preserved on any vintage.
- **The 30-vs-31 industry panel / exclusion list**: the exclusion key is **PRESENT** at `SalvagedInputs/book_data/Reconstructed/Shaikh_2008_Appendix_B_industries.csv` (31 excluded rows with `exclusion_ground` ∈ {non-profit-dominated, inadequate-WEQ-data, internationally-noncompetitive, low-or-negative-period-average}), extracted verbatim from **Shaikh (2008) Table 9.A1, p. 190**. This resolves `CH7_RESEARCH_SUMMARY.md` open-question #2. The 30 retained industries = the NAICS private industries *not* in that CSV. (Review M3/touchpoint: RSCD **inherits** the exclusion by reading Shaikh's already-reduced panel; no loader applies the CSV as a filter — the registry `components[]` listing the exclusion + adjustments overstates the code path but the DPR discloses the inheritance honestly.)
- **BEA IO benchmark-year re-reporting**: GDPbyIndustry is periodically re-anchored to the quinquennial benchmark I-O accounts (`IO_CHANGE_TIMELINE.md`: 1997 first NAICS benchmark; 2002/2007/2012/2017), each shifting summary/detail industry order — so industry indices are **not stable across benchmark years**.
- **NIPA comprehensive-revision exposure** (`_timelines/NIPA_CHANGE_TIMELINE.md`): Shaikh fixes the **2008 (≈2011) vintage**. The **2013** comprehensive revision **capitalizes R&D/IP → raises Fixed-Asset/capital-stock levels** (the S705 denominator); the **2018** revision inserts a **+1 line shift in NIPA T7.11** (resolver `Technical/docs/methodology/NIPA_T711_FISIM_remap.md`) and re-reports GDPbyIndustry; the **2023** revision re-references to 2017. Any extension past 2005 must be re-computed end-to-end on a single vintage with R&D/IP **excluded** to preserve Shaikh's 2008 capital concept — never spliced (CH7 open-question 5).

## 5. Replication fidelity note
S705 is a **byte-exact transcription of Shaikh's already-adjusted, already-reduced 30-industry panel** (V03 MAE 0.0, tol 1.0%, n=608), not an end-to-end recompute — the WEQ/OOH/inventory/reserve arithmetic and the exclusion live inside `Appendix7_ropdataUSind.xlsx`, authored by Shaikh. This is the *most faithful* reproduction available (identical to the published figure; figure-overlay MATCH per FIGURE_REPRO_ch07) but it means RSCD's registry `components[]` and `construction: composite` describe **Shaikh's** pipeline, not RSCD code (review M3 — faithful, but do not read the component list as an executed recipe). Honesty items: `reference_values` are round-trip-circular against the salvaged xlsx (auditability only, Decision 0002), and the token single-value pick (1987 = 0.07701 is the first industry "Accom", not the aggregate — review L1); the genuine book tie is the figure overlay. Registry `content_type: cross_sectional` contradicts the EPR's `time_series` (review M1) — the series has a real annual axis.

## 6. Forward risk
Highest-exposure series in the chapter for extension. The next **BEA benchmark/NAICS re-classification** (2022-NAICS already staged; a 2027 benchmark will follow) will re-order the 30-industry sample — re-run through the ADR-005 stability table. The **post-2013 R&D/IP capitalization** is a standing trap: an extension that silently adopts current Fixed Assets changes the capital concept and breaks comparability with Shaikh's 2008 rate. The Flow-of-Funds line numbers (L.109/L.114–117) have drifted in the current Z.1 release. Mitigation: any post-2005 extension re-fetches GVA/GOS/EC + Fixed Asset + T7.12 + Z.1 components on one coherent vintage, re-applies all four adjustments, re-excludes per Table 9.A1, and re-computes aggregate-before-ratio — with the vintage stamped.
