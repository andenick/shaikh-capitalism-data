## 1. What it is

S309 is the **luxury-good (`x2`) twin of S308**: the composite behind Figure 3.11 — one theoretical curve plus
four micro-foundations models — for the luxury good, price `p2` swept `2.0 → 3.0` (`x2` axis ~30–50). Five
subseries `S309-A…E`, 505 chopped rows (5 × 101). Together S308 (necessary) and S309 (luxury) with Table 3.1
are the chapter's capstone: the luxury demand elasticity `ex2,p2 ≈ −1.00 to −1.04` is essentially identical
across the theoretical baseline and all four behavioural models (`ch03_table_3.1.csv`;
`research.methodology_notes`), completing Shaikh's proof that *"the very different micro foundations… have
essentially no effect on the aggregate results"* (`book_quotes[0]`, p. 99).

## 3. Why these sources — Shaikh's rationale + rejected alternatives (and a provenance-label correction)

- **Why the luxury good separately from the necessary good.** Shaikh's whole consumer model rests on the
  *asymmetry* between the necessary good (with a minimum `x1min`, income elasticity < 1) and the luxury good
  (no minimum, income elasticity > 1). S308 shows the necessary good has `ex1,p1 ≈ −0.93`; S309 shows the luxury
  good sits at the unit-elastic benchmark `ex2,p2 ≈ −1`. The pair *is* Engel's Law in demand-curve form.
- **Why the same four models + book-stated constants + price sweep.** Identical rationale to S308: hold every
  parameter fixed so the only difference across curves is the behavioural micro-foundation, isolating it as the
  sole variable (`book_quotes`, pp. 99–100).
- **PROVENANCE-LABEL CORRECTION (F-CH3-03, HIGH — do not propagate the mislabel).** As with S308, the RSCD build
  did **not** run the NetLogo models (source unarchived, seeds unstated → exact Monte-Carlo repro impossible,
  `research.open_questions`). S309-B…E are **analytic × fixed per-model scalars**, not simulation output, despite
  the `NETLOGO_SIMS` subsource and the "read off printed figure" construction step. The honest description:
  **S309-A = analytic (eq. 3.6); S309-B…E = analytic × a fixed scalar, consistent with the printed spread; no
  simulation was run.** This MHR records the true construction; the mislabel is a defect to fix.
- **Rejected alternative — re-implementing NetLogo.** Feasible from the verbal specs but would not reproduce the
  printed curves exactly (unstated seeds); faithful near-copies were chosen over unfaithful fresh draws.
