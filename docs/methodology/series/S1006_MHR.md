## 3. Why these sources — Shaikh's rationale + rejected alternatives

- **Why *total returns* (Ibbotson), not yields.** The chapter's cross-asset point is about *realized total
  return* — capital gains plus income — across asset classes, which is what generates the equity-premium
  gap. A yield series (coupon or dividend yield, as in S1005) would miss the capital-gain component that
  dominates equity's realized return. Ibbotson SBBI Table 2-2 is the canonical US asset-class *total-return*
  series, standard in finance and pension-actuarial work (`research.methodology_notes[0]`;
  `research.book_quotes[0]`).
- **Why *nominal* returns, deliberately not real-deflated.** Shaikh states the point explicitly in the
  caption: subtracting a *common* inflation rate from all three would not change the evident difference
  between them (`research.book_quotes[0]`). Real-deflating would add a step without changing the rank
  ordering — so he leaves them nominal to keep the comparison transparent
  (`research.methodology_notes[3]`).
- **Why Ibbotson's ~20-year long bond indices.** Both the corporate and government bond lines are long-
  maturity (~20yr) total-return indices, matching the "long bond" horizon that the rest of the chapter uses
  (S1002's long yield), so the equity/bond/govt comparison is on a consistent long-horizon basis
  (`S1006_DPR.md` §1).
- **Rejected alternative — FRED SP500 as the equity line.** FRED `SP500` is a *price* index, not a
  total-return series, so it cannot stand in for Ibbotson's `rslarge`; a proper total-return series is
  required (`research.extension_candidates[1].concerns`).
- **The licensing-driven extension choice — Damodaran (NYU).** Ibbotson SBBI is now a **Morningstar
  commercial product** (`research.primary_source.license`; `research.primary_source.replaced_by`). For the
  *open* modern extension Shaikh's numbers are continued with Aswath Damodaran's free NYU reconstruction,
  which "largely replicates Ibbotson methodology" (`research.extension_candidates[0]`;
  `research.open_questions[0]`).

## 4. Methodological-change exposure — licensing + one proxy seam (KEY SECTION)

S1006 has **no NIPA line-number exposure**; its two exposures are a *licensing* boundary and a *proxy* seam:

- **Ibbotson→Damodaran at 2011 (the licensing seam).** Book period ends at the last freely-available Ibbotson
  year (2010); 2011+ uses Damodaran's `histretSP.html` for `S1006-A` (S&P 500 total return) and `S1006-C`
  (10-yr T.Bond) (`S1006_DPR.md` §3–§4; `CH10_RESEARCH_SUMMARY.md` Phase-5 S1006). Damodaran replicates
  Ibbotson's method with only small definitional differences in the corporate-bond index
  (`research.extension_candidates[0].concerns`).
- **The LT-corporate extension is a flagged PROXY.** Damodaran does **not** publish a long-corporate
  total-return series, so `S1006-B-ext` reconstructs an approximate total return from the **FRED `AAA`
  yield** (treating the yield as the running-yield component of a duration-stable LT-corporate index) —
  flagged `proxy: true` with a Concept Match Justification, and given a *distinct unit string* (`percent
  (yield, proxy for total return)`) so a yield is never silently mixed with a total return
  (`S1006_DPR.md` §7.2; registry `S1006-B-ext`; `CH10_review` D5 basis). A second open alternate,
  `S1006-B-ext-damodaran` (Damodaran Baa total return), is emitted alongside for sensitivity
  (`CH10_RESEARCH_SUMMARY.md` Phase-5 S1006).
- **No comprehensive-revision exposure.** These are market total returns, untouched by the 2013/2018/2023
  NIPA updates (`NIPA_CHANGE_TIMELINE.md`).
