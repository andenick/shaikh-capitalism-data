## 1. What it is (book definition + figure)

S1604 is the annual **net** rate of profit (gross corporate profit rate minus the 3-month T-bill rate) and
the **net real incremental** rate of profit, US Corporations, 1947–2011, on a decimal-rate scale. It is
**Figure 16.8**. Shaikh's own narrative (`research.book_quotes[0]`, p. 734): *"Combining the two gives us the
net rates of profit displayed in figure 16.8. We now see two things: first, that the Stagflation Crisis of
the late 1960s was precipitated when both rates sank to unprecedented lows. The whole behavior of the system
changed at this point…"*

S1604 is the chapter's central empirical exhibit for the **profitability-driven** character of crises: the
*net* (post-interest) profit rate and its counterfactual quantify how much of the post-1980s profit-rate
recovery was propped up by held-down real wages rather than genuine profitability gains (`S1604_DPR.md` §2).
It also emits the wage-suppression-adjusted variant `rcorpalt`, which consumes S1602's counterfactual EC.

## 3. Why these sources — Shaikh's rationale + rejected alternatives

- **Why the *net* rate (profit rate minus the T-bill).** The T-bill is the risk-free financial return; the
  net rate is the *excess* of productive profitability over the cost/opportunity of finance, and Fig 16.8's
  headline point is that both the net average and net incremental rates *"sank to unprecedented lows"*
  simultaneously in the late-1960s Stagflation Crisis (`research.book_quotes[0]`; `S1604_DPR.md` §2). The
  subtraction is not cosmetic — it is what makes the crisis timing visible.
- **Why the incremental rate, HP100-smoothed and lagged.** The *incremental* (marginal) profit rate leads
  the average; smoothing with HP100 and lagging one period (`S1604-D`) aligns it as the leading indicator
  Shaikh reads against the average rate (`research.methodology_notes[1]`; `S1604_DPR.md` §4).
- **Why the counterfactual `rcorpalt`.** The chapter's argument that post-1980s profitability was sustained
  by wage suppression *requires* a wage-suppression-free profit rate. `rcorpalt` re-prices corporate labour
  cost on S1602's counterfactual EC path and recomputes the profit rate — the difference from the actual
  rate is the wage-suppression contribution (`research.methodology_notes[2]`; `S1604_DPR.md` §1; `S1604_EPR.md` §3).
- **Why λ = 100 for the HP filter (Phase 4 ratified, canonical).** Shaikh uses **λ = 100** for annual data;
  RSCD pins it as a module constant (`HP_LAMBDA`) and emits the Ravn–Uhlig **λ = 6.25** only as a
  *sensitivity sidecar*, NOT the Fig 16.8 series (`research.open_questions[1]`; `S1604_DPR.md` §4 + §7 caveat 1;
  `CH16_RESEARCH_SUMMARY.md` Phase-4 note). λ = 6.25 is the explicitly **rejected** canonical alternative.
- **Rejected — CD2-frozen profit-rate values.** S1604 must consume RSCD-canonical S0608 outputs, not CD2's
  frozen `r_corp`, to avoid double-counting in the cross-series uniqueness check (`S1604_DPR.md` §7 caveat 4;
  `research.open_questions[0]`).

## 4. Methodological-change exposure (NIPA vintage + benchmark I-O — KEY SECTION)

Uniquely in ch16, S1604's inputs run **deep into the BEA NIPA + Fixed Assets + benchmark-I-O machinery**
through the chapter-6 profit-rate chain, so it inherits the full comprehensive-update exposure documented in
the Phase-0 timelines. Shaikh fixes all BEA data at the **~2011 vintage** (Appendix 6.7 fn 1;
`NIPA_CHANGE_TIMELINE.md` §"Why this matters"):

- **2013 Comprehensive Update (14th).** R&D and entertainment/artistic originals **capitalized** → new IPP
  category, **≈ +$400B to GDP** and a **rise in the fixed-capital stock** plus CFC/NOS restatement
  (`NIPA_CHANGE_TIMELINE.md` 2013 row). Both the profit numerator (corporate profits/NOS) and the profit
  denominator (net capital stock) move — so `r_corp` moves *by construction*. Incorporated the **2007
  benchmark I-O**.
- **2018 Comprehensive Update (15th).** 2012 benchmark I-O; financial-services (FISIM) method changes;
  **T7.11 +1 line shift** — the classic hard-coded-line-number time-bomb for the chapter-6 FISIM recipe
  (`NIPA_CHANGE_TIMELINE.md` §"Table-renumbering"; resolver `NIPA_T711_FISIM_remap.md`).
- **2023 Comprehensive Update (16th).** Reference year → 2017; chain-index levels re-based.
- **Benchmark-I-O touch.** Each NIPA comprehensive update *incorporates a benchmark I-O account* (2007→2013,
  2012→2018, 2017→2023; `IO_CHANGE_TIMELINE.md` benchmark table). S1604's upstream corporate profit-rate
  chain is therefore exposed to benchmark-I-O-driven revisions of industry NOS/CFC — an **indirect but real
  I-O touch**, distinct from S1601/S1602/S1603/S1605/S1606 which have none.
- **HP endpoint fragility (structural, non-NIPA).** Adding even one new annual observation shifts the *entire*
  HP100-smoothed `S1604-C/D` path — a known two-sided-filter property, flagged `hp100_endpoint_fragility: true`
  in the registry (`S1604_DPR.md` §4 + §7 caveat 2; `CH16_review.INFO2`). Any extension must **re-derive on
  one coherent vintage**, never splice the difference series or the smoothed columns across a
  comprehensive-revision boundary.

  deferred) and on RSCD-canonical S0606–S0608 outputs (`S1604_DPR.md` §7 caveat 3; `S1604_EPR.md` §3).

## 6. Forward risk

- **Re-derive on ONE coherent NIPA vintage (never splice across 2013/2018/2023).** The profit-rate inputs
  straddle three comprehensive updates; a live re-fetch that crosses a boundary mixes vintages of both the
  profit numerator and the capital-stock denominator. Pin ALFRED/vintage and re-run the chapter-6 chain
  end-to-end (`NIPA_CHANGE_TIMELINE.md` §"Why this matters"; `research.open_questions` cross-ref).
- **Apply the T7.11 line-shift resolver.** Any 2018+ vintage of the FISIM inputs must resolve by BEA
  `LineDescription` label, not the 2011-vintage line numbers, per `NIPA_T711_FISIM_remap.md`.
- **HP endpoint fragility.** Publish the smoothed columns with the `hp100_endpoint_fragility` flag; recompute
  HP100 on the full extended sample and warn downstream consumers that smoothed values are not stable across
  runs (`S1604_DPR.md` §7 caveat 2; `CH16_review.INFO2`).
- **Cross-series ordering + uniqueness.** Enforce S1602 → S1604 (`rcorpalt` needs `ec_c`) and consume
  RSCD-canonical S0608 (not CD2-frozen) to avoid double-counting (`S1604_DPR.md` §7 caveats 3–4;
  `research.open_questions[0]`).
