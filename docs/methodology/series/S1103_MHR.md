## 1. What it is (book definition + Appendix 11.1 + figure)

S1103 is the pair behind **Figure 11.6** (book p. 531): for the US (S1103-B) and Japan (S1103-A),
1960–2009, the **ratio of the PPI-basis REER to the adjusted real unit labour cost ratio** —
`rxrrulcratio1 = rxr1 / rulcadjratio1rescaled` — a dimensionless index scaled so ~100 = parity. Shaikh's
caption (`research.book_quotes[1]`, p. 531) is *"Law of One Price at the Aggregate Level, United States and
Japan, 1960–2009"*, and his interpretive sentence (`book_quotes[0]`, p. 531) states the finding: *"the
appropriate aggregate test of the LOP is to look at the deviation of the real exchange rate from its
fundamentals … it is remarkable how stable this ratio is over the long run,"* despite *"the large impact of
the anomalous 1980–1987 period."*

Values ship from **Appendix 11.1**, column `rxrrulcratio1` of `Appendix11_USJPNdata.xlsx` (`S1103_DPR.md`
§3). Fig 11.6 is the **payoff exhibit of the chapter**: having shown REERs are non-stationary (S1102),
Shaikh shows the REER *divided by its RULC fundamental* is long-run stable — i.e. real exchange rates
gravitate around relative real unit labour costs. This is the classical "law of one price at the aggregate
level."

## 2. Source lineage (a derived ratio of two Shaikh-computed series)

S1103 is not a raw observation; it is a **ratio of two other author-computed series**
(`research.methodology_notes[0-1]`; `S1103_DPR.md` §4), fixed by the Appendix 11 Documentation sheet as
`rxrrulcratio1 = rxr1 / rulcadjratio1rescaled`:

| Component | Definition | Underlying sources (book vintage) |
|---|---|---|
| **Numerator** `rxr1` — REER PPI-basis | `= (ppi·e)/ppi*`, eq. 11.16 | **= S1102** (BLS ILC Table 11 e, WDI PPI, IMF IFS weights) |
| **Denominator** `rulcadjratio1rescaled` — adjusted RULC ratio | `(RULC_c/RULC*_c)·(τ_c/τ*_c)`, rescaled to REER period average | **BLS ILC Table 9** ULC, BLS/FRED CPI, **WDI** PPI |

where (Appendix 11.1.III, `research.book_quotes[2]`, p. 876) `RULC = ULC/CPI` and `τ = CPI/PPI` is the
non-tradable/tradable composition adjustment. Formula (`research.formula`; `S1103_DPR.md` §1):
`LOP_agg_{c,t} = REER_PPI / [(RULC_c/RULC*_c)·(τ_c/τ*_c)]`. Construction was **ratified `direct → formula`**
in Phase 4 precisely so the extension is forced to re-compute the ratio from re-extended parts, not
lazy-splice it (`CH11_ADEQUACY_REPORT` Q6; `S1103_DPR.md` §4).

## 3. Why these sources — the vertically-integrated real-unit-labour-cost concept and its specific sources

This is the conceptual heart of Chapter 11, and it contains **Shaikh's own in-book proxy** (distinct from
the post-2009 extension proxy in §4).

- **Why divide the REER by relative RULC at all — the LOP fundamental.** Shaikh's classical theory says the
  real exchange rate is not anchored by PPP (S1102 disproves that) but by **relative real unit labour
  costs** — the true regulator of competitive prices of production. Fig 11.6 tests this by taking the ratio:
  if the REER tracks its RULC fundamental, the ratio is stationary near parity. So the denominator is not an
  arbitrary deflator — it *is* the classical fundamental (`S1103_DPR.md` §2; `research.methodology_notes[2]`).
- **Why VERTICALLY-INTEGRATED real unit labour cost — and Shaikh's explicit data-limitation proxy.** Shaikh
  states the *intended* concept verbatim (`research.book_quotes[2]`, p. 876): the variables *"(vulcr/vulcr*)·
  (τ/τ*), where vulcr, vulcr* are proxies for the **vertically integrated real unit labor costs** of a
  country and its trading partners (we use direct real unit labor costs RULC = ULC/CPI and RULC* **due to
  lack of data on vertically integrated costs**)."* The proper measure is the *vertically integrated* real
  unit labour cost — total (direct + indirect, through the input chain) labour cost per unit of tradable
  output — because that is what governs a country's competitive price. Lacking full input-output-integrated
  labour-cost data across the panel, **Shaikh himself substitutes direct RULC = ULC/CPI as an explicit,
  disclosed proxy** for vertically-integrated RULC. This is a *book-period, author-declared* proxy over an
  **absent integrated-labour-cost source** — it is NOT the post-2009 extension proxy and must not be
  confused with it.
- **Why the τ = CPI/PPI tradable/non-tradable adjustment.** Direct RULC is deflated by CPI (a consumption
  basket), but the competitive-price concept is about tradables (producer prices). The `τ = CPI/PPI` factor
  re-weights the RULC ratio for the differing non-tradable/tradable composition of each country, aligning
  the denominator with the PPI-deflated numerator (`research.methodology_notes` on τ; `S1103_DPR.md` §3).
  Its CPI and WDI-PPI inputs remain on active sources — **not substituted** even in the extension
  (`S1103_EPR.md` §2).
- **Why manufacturing ULC from BLS ILC Table 9.** Same rationale as S1102's e: the BLS ILC program uniquely
  delivered internationally-harmonised *manufacturing* (tradables) ULC across the sample. Whole-economy ULC
  would dilute the tradables concept — which is exactly why the OECD whole-economy substitute in §4 carries
  a sectoral-narrowing caveat.
- **Why rescale the denominator to the REER period average.** `rulcadjratio1` is rescaled to share the
  REER's period-average level so the ratio is dimensionally interpretable as a stability test around ~100
  (`research.methodology_notes[4]`; `S1103_DPR.md` caveat). The rescale is a leveling convention, not a data
  operation.

## 4. The BLS ILC → BIS + OECD/Conference Board proxy — concept-match and TEMPORAL SCOPE (KEY SECTION)

**The extension proxy applies to the deferred post-2009 period ONLY; the 1960–2009 book-period chart is
Shaikh's own Appendix 11.1 `rxrrulcratio1`, verified bit-exact (§5).** S1103 requires a **coordinated
dual-proxy** because BOTH its numerator and denominator trace to the BLS ILC program discontinued in 2013
(`S1103_DPR.md` §7 caveat 2; `S1103_EPR.md` §1). Note the registry's `proxy_justification` leading clause
omits an explicit "post-2009" tag — the review flags this wording gap (F3) that this MHR corrects.

- **Numerator (REER).** Inherits S1102's substitution: **BIS PPI-based EER broad index**, concept-match
  ACCEPTABLE (trade-weighted multilateral PPI-deflated REER; basket/weighting differ, concept identical) —
  see `S1102_MHR.md` §4 and `S1103_EPR.md` §2.
- **Denominator (adjusted RULC) — dual variant strategy** (`S1103_EPR.md` §2; `CH11_ADEQUACY_REPORT`
  chapter recommendation 2):
  - **Open-license path `S1103_open`:** **OECD ULC (`ULC_QUA`)**. Concept-match **partial**: OECD is
    *whole-economy* (ISIC A–T) whereas Shaikh used *manufacturing-only* BLS ILC — a documented
    sectoral-narrowing gap, flagged `proxy:true` with a sectoral note. CC-BY, ships freely
    (`CH11_ADEQUACY_REPORT.data_substitutions_recorded[1].concept_match_justification_open`).
  - **High-fidelity path `S1103_hifi`:** **Conference Board International Labor Comparisons** — the explicit
    *successor program* to BLS ILC (manufacturing ULC, documented method-continuity). **Direct** concept
    match, but requires Conference Board membership/license, so it is **not shippable in the open-license
    replication package** (`CH11_ADEQUACY_REPORT.data_substitutions_recorded[1].concept_match_justification_hifi`).
  - **τ adjustment (CPI/PPI): NOT substituted** — BLS/FRED CPI and WDI PPI remain active
    (`S1103_EPR.md` §2).
- **Rejected alternative.** A single lazy growth-rate splice of the published `rxrrulcratio1` ratio — rejected
  by the Phase-4 `formula` reclassification, which mandates re-computing numerator and denominator
  separately with consistent splice years (`CH11_ADEQUACY_REPORT` Q6; `S1103_DPR.md` §4).
