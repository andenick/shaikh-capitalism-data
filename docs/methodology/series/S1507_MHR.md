## 1. What it is (book definition + Appendix 15.1 + figure)

S1507 is the **post-neoliberal (1982–2010) sub-period** of the Classical/Conventional Phillips scatter,
**Figure 15.9** (`research.book_quotes[1]`, p. 710). Like S1506 it is the *same data* as S1505 restricted to a
window — here 1982–2010 (`research.formula`; registry `construction: derived_subperiod`). Shaikh's framing
(`book_quotes[0]`, p. 708): *"The contrast … becomes even starker when we look separately at the pre- and
post-neoliberal eras."* He ties the regime shift to the Reagan/Volcker-era *"weakening of labor and of the
welfare state"* that *"lowered the intercept of the wage-share Phillips curve and made its slope less steep
(figure 14.14)"* (`book_quotes[2]`, p. 709) — linking this chapter's Phillips curves back to Ch14 (S1405).

Unlike the closed S1506, S1507 is the **open post-Volcker window** — the natural forward-extension target when
the parent S1505 extends, since the neoliberal regime is still ongoing (`research.methodology_notes[0]`;
`S1507_DPR.md`).

## 3. Why these sources — Shaikh's rationale + rejected alternatives

- **Why slice rather than rebuild** — identical to S1506 §3: the regime-contrast argument requires *the same
  variable definitions* on both sides of the 1982 break, so S1507 reads the parent parquet rather than
  re-deriving π, σ, uL.
- **Why 1982 as the break, not 1981.** Shaikh dates the regime change to the **1982 Volcker-shock disinflation**,
  not the 1981 Reagan inauguration (`research.open_questions[0]`); the pre-side (S1506) ends 1981 and the
  post-side (S1507) begins 1982, so the two windows tile 1948–2010 with no gap or overlap.
- **Rejected: pooling 1948–2010 into one curve.** The whole point of Figs 15.8/15.9 is that the pooled S1505
  scatter hides a regime shift; Shaikh deliberately splits it (`research.book_quotes[0]`).
- Parent-level rejections (CPI for π, corporate-profit denominator for σ, conventional curve as truth) carry over
  from S1505 §3.

## 4. Methodological-change exposure (KEY SECTION)

- **Inherits the parent NIPA vintage exposure — and, being the OPEN window, is the one that actually extends.**
  S1507 is the natural post-1982 extension target (`research.extension_candidates[0]`), so its σ (T5.3.5 line 2 /
  T1.16 line 2) and π (T1.1.4 deflator) carry the full 2013/2018/2023 exposure *at the moment of extension*: the
  2013 IPP capitalization and NOS/profit restatement move the σ denominator; 2023's reference-year rebasing
  (→2017) moves the deflator behind π (`NIPA_CHANGE_TIMELINE.md`). Because the neoliberal regime is ongoing, an
  extension **must re-derive the whole parent on one coherent vintage** and re-slice — never append post-2010
  points onto the frozen book-vintage parent.
- **T1.16 line-order drift** applies (resolve σ's profit line by BEA `LineDescription` label; `NIPA_CHANGE_TIMELINE.md`).
- **Handfas-2012 σ dependency** must be honored in any extension (no corporate-profits FRED proxy).
- **Cross-series:** parent S1505 → {S1506 closed, S1507 open}; Handfas (2012)'s econometric tests rely precisely
  on this 1982-break sub-period dummy structure (`research.methodology_notes[2]`).

  coherent post-2011 NIPA vintage and re-slice 1982→present; never splice σ across the 2013/2018/2023 boundaries
