## 1. What the series is
S711 is Shaikh's **OECD-wide incremental-rate-of-profit deviation panel**, 1988–2003: for ~27 industries, dev_i,t = IROP_i,t − IROP_avg,t, where the underlying IROP is built from **PPP-adjusted, cross-country-aggregated** OECD data. Book definition (page-cited, `S711_research.json`, p. 305): *"more recent OECD data can be used to extend earlier investigations. Data on capital stock was too sparse to calculate average rates of profits for the OECD as a whole, but it was possible to calculate corresponding incremental rates of profit … Figure 7.21 displays the deviations of incremental profit rates from their overall mean … The patterns are the same as in the previous case: incremental rates of profit cross back and forth a great deal."* It is the **third tier** of the empirical case (US → Greek → OECD-wide) and is **IROP-only** — there is deliberately no OECD analogue of Figs 7.15/7.16 because STAN carries no capital stock. S711 also **resolves the long-standing S707/S038 alias collision** (Decision 0004): the OECD content the original S707 stub mislabeled now lives here.

## 2. Source lineage
- **Primary agency data**: **OECD STructural ANalysis (STAN) Database, 2003 vintage** (Appendix 7.1 §V.1, book p. 858), ISIC Rev 3. Variables: **GFCF** (gross fixed capital formation = gross investment) and **GOPS** (gross operating surplus & mixed income = gross profit; where not reported directly, constructed as **NOPS + CFC**). ~30 OECD countries.
- **PPP conversion**: **Penn World Table 6.2** (Heston-Summers-Aten 2006) PPP factors, converting each country's local-currency (or euro, post-EMU) GFCF/GOPS to **PPP-adjusted US dollars ("International Dollars")** — book p. 859 §V.6.
- **Aggregation / adjustment chain** (formula in `S711_research.json`): (a) PPP-convert each country-industry GFCF/GOPS; (b) **sum across countries per industry** (aggregate-before-ratio), restricted to industries with **3+ countries** reporting both variables (§V.4); (c) IROP_i,t = (GOPS_i,t − GOPS_i,t−1)/GFCF_i,t−1; (d) IROP_avg,t across included industries; (e) dev_i,t = IROP_i,t − IROP_avg,t. **NO WEQ adjustment** — international data did not allow removal of self-employed income (book p. 859 §V.2), a known cross-country comparability limitation vs the US series. Same arts/education/law/medicine exclusion *logic* as the US (§V.5).
- **Native units**: rate deviation (decimal); underlying IROP in PPP International Dollars. Data ends **2003** (registry-correct; Fig 7.21 plots 1988–2003).
- **RSCD transcription vehicle**: Shaikh's **Appendix 7.2 sheet `iropOECDPPP`** (book p. 859) — the fully-aggregated, PPP-converted derived panel is transcribed byte-exact (V03 MAE 0.0, tol 1.0%, n=448); RSCD does not re-run the STAN aggregation.

## 3. Why these sources (Shaikh's perspective) + rejected alternatives
Shaikh reaches for OECD STAN + PWT because he wants to test equalization at the **broadest competitive arena — developed-world industry as a whole** — using *more recent* data than the discontinued ISDB behind Figs 7.13/7.14. He accepts a deliberate asymmetry: STAN provides investment and profit but **no capital stock**, so only the **incremental** rate is computable (book p. 305) — which suits him, since IROP is the measure he trusts most for equalization anyway. PPP conversion via PWT is essential so that summing GFCF/GOPS across ~30 currencies yields a coherent world-industry aggregate. He explicitly chose the **2003 STAN vintage because it covered ~30 countries**, warning (book p. 859 §V.3) that *"the subsequent version … covered only eighteen countries and excluded even those such as Canada and the United Kingdom"* — a rare on-the-record statement of source-vintage discontinuity by the author himself.
**Rejected alternatives** (`S711_research.json`): OECD **ISDB** (rejected — discontinued, cf. S703/S704); current **STAN 2025** (usable only for extension, not the book series — reduced country coverage + ISIC Rev4); computing IROP as an *average of country-industry* IROPs (rejected — aggregate-before-ratio per §V.7); applying WEQ (impossible — no international self-employed data).

## 4. Methodological-change exposure — the chapter's richest international concordance case
S711 concentrates the chapter's international classification/vintage exposure (review touchpoint S711: "richest concordance exposure in the chapter"):
- **ISIC Rev 3 → Rev 4 break**: the book series is on **ISIC Rev 3** (STAN 2003). Any extension onto STAN 2025 requires an **ISIC Rev 3 → Rev 4 crosswalk** — the international analogue of the US SIC→NAICS wall — and some of Shaikh's idiosyncratic short labels ("Wood&publishing", "sale.motor", "Comp.act.") **do not map cleanly** (`S711_research.json` open-question 2). RSCD stages only US Census SIC↔NAICS bridges (`_sources/naics/`); **no ISIC Rev3↔Rev4 concordance is staged** — it must be built for any extension.
- **Country-coverage discontinuity (author-documented)**: 30-country (2003) → 18-country (later STAN, excl. Canada/UK) shrinkage (book p. 859 §V.3). The world-industry aggregate's *country composition* changes across vintages, so a spliced deviation series would compare non-comparable aggregates.
- **Post-1997 capital-flow / capital-stock gap**: STAN's absence of capital stock is the international face of the same obstacle documented for the US in `IO_CHANGE_TIMELINE.md` (benchmark capital-flow matrix discontinued after 1997) — it is *why* only IROP is computable OECD-wide.
- **PPP vintage**: PWT 6.2 → 10.01 methodology shift (mostly washes out within an industry's PPP-weighted totals, but must be re-run for any extension).
- **No US NIPA exposure** for the book series (STAN, not NIPA); the `NIPA_CHANGE_TIMELINE.md` bears only on the US members' national-accounts inputs upstream of STAN.
CD2 recorded that a STAN 2025 + PWT 10.01 re-aggregation with a careful crosswalk reproduced the overlap window at **mean-deviation MAE ≈ 0.04** — i.e. methodological continuity is *achievable* but contingent on the crosswalk (`S711_research.json` methodology_notes; CH7 open-question).

## 5. Replication fidelity note
S711 is a **byte-exact transcription of Shaikh's already-aggregated `iropOECDPPP` derived panel** (V03 MAE 0.0, tol 1.0%, n=448; figure-overlay MATCH per FIGURE_REPRO_ch07), not an end-to-end re-run of the STAN country aggregation + PPP conversion — so the registry `construction: composite` and the multi-step formula describe **Shaikh's** pipeline (STAN → PWT → aggregate → IROP → deviation), which RSCD faithfully reproduces at the *output* level rather than re-executing. `reference_values` are round-trip-circular (Decision 0002); registry `content_type: cross_sectional` contradicts the EPR's `time_series` (review M1). The display name was corrected to 1988–2003 to match the data (the "1988–2005" framing refers to the data-vintage envelope). The **no-WEQ** limitation is disclosed honestly (§2) — the OECD IROP is not strictly comparable to the WEQ-cleaned US IROP.

## 6. Forward risk
Highest international extension risk in the chapter. A STAN 2025 extension must absorb **three** simultaneous breaks: the **ISIC Rev 3 → Rev 4** reclassification (build the crosswalk RSCD does not yet stage), the **30→18 country-coverage collapse** (explicit Concept Match Justification required per book p. 859 §V.3), and the **PWT 6.2 → 10.01** PPP change. The 2003 STAN vintage is no longer canonically hosted, so re-fetch is from STAN 2025 (different industry list/coverage) — never a splice. Any extension re-aggregates countries → recomputes IROP → recomputes deviations end-to-end on one coherent vintage, stamps it, and carries the country-list + crosswalk as provenance. Deferred to Phase 4+ per Decision 0005.
