## 1. What it is (book definition + figure)

S1008 is the three-line, two-panel comparison behind **Figure 10.13** (book p. 473), 1947–2011: the **actual
real stock price** `preq`, **Shiller's "rational" price P*** re-deflated to the BEA basis `prstarshiller1`,
and the **classical warranted price** `prweq` from equation (10.31). Shaikh's framing
(`research.book_quotes[1]`, role=definition, p. 473): *"The first panel … compares the actual real stock
price to the estimated rational price whose smoothness is the source of Shiller's concerns about the EMH. The
second panel … displays the actual real stock price alongside the classical warranted rate."* Ships as
`S1008-A` (actual real), `S1008-B` (Shiller P* re-deflated), `S1008-C` (classical warranted) — all
`index (real, BEA gross investment deflator)` (registry).

Figure 10.13 is Shaikh's **central anti-EMH visual**: the classical warranted price — driven by the
*profit rate* — tracks the actual price's long swings (1950s–60s boom, 1970s downturn, dot-com bubble),
whereas Shiller's constant-discount-rate P* is monotone-smooth and misses them
(`CH10_RESEARCH_SUMMARY.md` "S1008 …"; `research.methodology_notes[0]`).

## 2. Source lineage (Shiller P/P* + BEA deflator + eq. 10.31 recursion on S1007's rI)

Fixed verbatim in Appendix 10.1 (p. 874, `research.book_quotes[2]`, role=source): *"The actual real stock
price is Shiller's nominal price deflated by the BEA gross investment deflator … The classical warranted
price was derived in chapter 10, section VI, according to … equation (10.31) … Shiller's own 'rational'
stock price is from his website … under the name P* (Present Value of Real Dividends, Const r)."*

| Input | Source | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Shiller nominal S&P price `P` | Shiller `ie_data.xls`, col P | `preq` numerator |
| Shiller P* (PV of real dividends, const r=7.6%) + Shiller CPI | Shiller `ie_data.xls` | `prstarshiller1` |
| BEA gross investment deflator | **NIPA T1.1.9** / App. 6.8.II.7 (via S1007) | real-deflation of all three |
| `rI` corporate incremental rate of profit | **S1007-B** (adjusted IROP) | drives eq. 10.31 |
| `dvr` real dividends | Shiller `dv` / BEA deflator | eq. 10.31 subtrahend |

Formula (`research.formula`; eq. 10.31, `research.book_quotes[0]`): `preq_t = P_nom_t / deflator_t`;
`prstarshiller1_t = (P*_t · CPI_t/100) / deflator_t`; `prweq_t = prweq_{t-1}·(1 + rI_t) − dvr_t`, with the
initial value chosen so `prweq` has the same mean as `preq` over 1948–2011.

## 3. Why these sources — Shaikh's rationale + rejected alternatives

- **Why the warranted price is driven by `rI` (the profit rate), NOT a constant discount rate (KEY concept).**
  Equation (10.31) is a forward recursion in which each year's warranted price grows at the *incremental rate
  of profit* `rI` (from S1007) and pays out real dividends — so the warranted price depends *entirely* on the
  regulating profit rate, not on a fixed r (`research.book_quotes[0]`; `research.methodology_notes[3]`). This
  is the whole anti-EMH argument: Shiller's P* assumes a constant discount rate (r=7.6%) and comes out
  smooth; Shaikh's profit-rate-driven price tracks the actual swings.
- **Why deflate *all three* prices by the BEA gross investment deflator (not CPI).** Commensurability, exactly
  as in S1007: the warranted price is generated by a current-cost profit rate on investment goods, so every
  price on the chart must be on the *same* investment-goods price basis. Shiller's P* is natively CPI-real, so
  Shaikh explicitly **un-deflates it by Shiller's CPI and re-deflates by the BEA gross investment index**
  (footnote 25, p. 473; `research.methodology_notes[2]`) — otherwise the "rational" and "warranted" lines
  would sit on incompatible price bases and the comparison would be meaningless.
- **Why the "same-mean" initialization (footnote 24).** Equation (10.31) is a recursion that needs a starting
  value, but the *level* of a warranted price is not pinned by the theory — only its *dynamics* are. Shaikh
  therefore fixes the initial `prweq` so it shares the mean of the actual price over 1948–2011 — a pure
  *normalization* to put the two lines on the same level for visual comparison, **not** a fitted model
  parameter (`research.book_quotes` footnote 24; `research.methodology_notes[1]`;
  `research.open_questions[0]`).
- **Rejected alternative — re-anchoring the initialization on the extended interval.** Phase 4 ruled the
  initialization must **not** be re-anchored on an extended sample; the workbook hardcodes the calibrated
  constant `adj = 6.75` (see §5), which the build pins (`CH10_RESEARCH_SUMMARY.md` Phase-5 S1008;
  open question 2).

## 4. Methodological-change exposure — inherited NIPA vintage (KEY SECTION)

S1008 has **no independent NIPA fetch** of its own beyond the deflator, but it inherits S1007's full NIPA
exposure through `rI` and the shared deflator:

- **Inherits S1007's `rI` (adjusted corporate IROP).** The warranted price is only as vintage-stable as
  S1007-B, which rests on T1.14 NOS + the T7.11 monetary-interest FISIM magnitude + T5.2.3/5.6.3 investment —
  exposed to the 2013 (NOS/FISIM restatement), 2018 (T7.11 +1 line shift), and 2023 (reference-year rebasing)
  comprehensive updates (`NIPA_CHANGE_TIMELINE.md`; see S1007 MHR §4). Because `prweq_t` is a *cumulative*
  recursion in `rI`, any error or vintage break in `rI` **compounds forward** through the whole warranted
  price path.
- **Shared BEA gross investment deflator (T1.1.9).** Re-based to 2017 in the 2023 update; all three lines are
  deflated by it, so a re-base rescales the entire panel (`NIPA_CHANGE_TIMELINE.md` 2023 row).
- **Shiller P* versioning.** Shiller's P* methodology changed across his 1981/2001/2014 papers (different
  constant discount rate each time); Shaikh uses the **2014 value r=7.6%**, so any refresh must confirm the
  downloaded P* column still matches the 2014 version (`research.open_questions[1]`).
- **Extension deferred.** S1008 inherits S1007's vintage-pinning deferral (`CH10_RESEARCH_SUMMARY.md`
  Phase-5 S1008). No ALFRED pin.

## 5. Replication fidelity note

- **Book period is exact.** V03 PASS, **MAE = 0, 196 chopped rows** over 1947–2011 (`preq`/`prstarshiller1`
  pass-through; `prweq` recomputed by eq. 10.31 forward iteration; `CH10_RESEARCH_SUMMARY.md` Phase-5 table).
  `reference_values` are circular; no non-circular in-book anchor (`CH10_review.findings.L5`).
- **The `adj = 6.75` discovery (honest pin).** The book's published `prweq` column hardcodes `adj = 6.75`,
  verified exactly by `prweq[1947] − preq[1947] = 72.052 − 65.302 = 6.75` (`CH10_RESEARCH_SUMMARY.md`
  Phase-5 S1008). Footnote 24's "same-mean over 1948–2011" describes the calibration that *produced* the
  original `adj`, but the workbook uses the calibrated value as a **fixed parameter** — which does not
  exactly satisfy mean-equality on the full panel. The build pins `BOOK_ADJ = 6.75` in P02 to reproduce the
  published column exactly and to honor the Phase-4 "must not re-anchor on the extended interval" ruling; the
  footnote's claim is thus a simplification of the original calibration, disclosed honestly
  (`CH10_RESEARCH_SUMMARY.md` open question 2).
- **P* re-deflation is the fragile step.** Re-converting Shiller's CPI-real P* to the BEA basis (footnote 25)
  is easy to get wrong and is flagged in the open questions (`research.open_questions`;
  `CH10_RESEARCH_SUMMARY.md` "Cross-references").
